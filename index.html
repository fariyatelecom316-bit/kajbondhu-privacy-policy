KajBondhu Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KajBondhu ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the KajBondhu application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may process the following information:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address.
  • Work-related information, such as your profession and phone number, if provided.
  • Job information, job applications, application status, and related account identifiers.
  • Payment-related records and reviews, if you use features that submit this information.
  • Information you submit when creating or managing a profile or using the app.

The specific information processed depends on the features available in the version of the app you use. We do not intend to collect information that is unnecessary for providing the app's services.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate users and manage accounts.
  • Display profiles and relevant work or service information.
  • Connect workers and people seeking services.
  • Manage job applications and their status.
  • Maintain relevant service, payment, and review records.
  • Respond to support requests and help protect the app from misuse.

3. Storage and Service Providers

KajBondhu uses Google Firebase services for certain app functions, including authentication and cloud database storage. Information processed by these services may be stored on their infrastructure and handled under the applicable service terms and privacy practices.

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers that help operate the app, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service.

4. Payments

Cash is the currently supported payment method where applicable. Digital payment options such as bKash, Nagad, and Rocket are not available unless they are explicitly enabled in a future version. Do not submit payment credentials for a payment method that is not supported by the app.

5. Data Security and Retention

We use the cloud services configured for the app to store and process information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

The current version of the app does not provide a self-service account deletion option. To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, email us at fariyatelecom316@gmail.com using the email address associated with your account.

We may need to verify your request before processing it. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and recordkeeping purposes. We will handle valid requests according to applicable requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

KajBondhu is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us using the email below.
