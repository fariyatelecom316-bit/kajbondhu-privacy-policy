KajBondhu Account Deletion

This page explains how KajBondhu users can request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion, send an email from the email address associated with your KajBondhu account to:

fariyatelecom316@gmail.com

Please include “KajBondhu Account Deletion Request” in the subject line.

What Happens Next?

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing your request. After verification, we will review and process the request in accordance with applicable requirements.

Data Deletion and Retention

We will review requests to delete the account and associated personal information. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, dispute-resolution, or recordkeeping purposes.

Important: Sending an email submits a deletion request; it does not mean the account has already been deleted. The request must be reviewed and processed.
